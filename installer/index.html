🕷 SpiderBridge

Spider Farmer GGS controller ⇄ Home Assistant. Everything the app can do, 1:1 in Home Assistant — no app, no cloud, installation and operation entirely without them.

1 · Flash the firmware

Connect the ESP32 board by USB (hold BOOT if the port does not appear), choose the serial port and click install. The firmware targets ESP32-WROOM-32 (4 MB) — developed and tested with this board.

The installer always uses the newest build of this repository. Alternatively join the hotspot SpiderBridge (password 12345678, http://192.168.10.1) and configure Wi-Fi manually.

2 · Send Wi-Fi credentials (during installation)

Immediately after flashing, the ESP32 stays in provisioning mode for 5 minutes. Keep this tab open, enter your home Wi-Fi below and click Connect — the credentials are sent to the ESP32 over the serial port (Improv Serial, no cables swapped, no browser extensions).

If the port disappears after flashing, close and reopen it — the Improv dialog needs the serial port itself. Without this step the bridge creates its own hotspot (SpiderBridge, password 12345678) and you can configure Wi-Fi at http://192.168.10.1.

3 · What happens next

  1. The bridge joins your network — open its web interface (hostname spiderbridge.local or its IP from your router).
  2. Enter your MQTT broker (Settings → MQTT).
  3. Pair the GGS controller over Bluetooth (Control → Bluetooth scan, or the Pair Bluetooth button in Home Assistant).
  4. Home Assistant discovers ~154 entities automatically — done. No app, no cloud.
